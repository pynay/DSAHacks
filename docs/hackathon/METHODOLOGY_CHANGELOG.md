# SD Downtown Homelessness - Methodology Change Log (exported from Drive gdoc)

How DSDP Clean & Safe counted unsheltered individuals and what changed when. The count is a visual street sweep: people in the open counted directly; **tents/structures and inhabited vehicles counted as a multiple of persons**. The multiplier is what changes.

## Timeline

| Period key | Effective | Tent | Vehicle | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PRE2017 | through 2017-04-26 | 2.00 | 2.00 | Preliminary in-house methodology |
| APR2017 | 2017-04-27 → 2018-05-30 | 1.75 | 1.66 | Adopted RTFH methodology |
| MAY2018 | 2018-05-31 → 2019-12-31 | 1.75 | 2.03 | RTFH raised the vehicle factor |
| POST2020 | 2020-01-01 → present | 1.75 | 2.03 | **RTFH abandoned multipliers; C&S continued** |

Individuals always 1:1.

- **PRE2017**: 18 rows (Jan–Mar 2017) publish total only; 2.00/2.00 cannot be unwound. Not on the same basis as later months.
- **APR2017**: downward revision for both tents and vehicles ("1.75 individuals per visible tent/structure and 1.66 per vehicle showing clear signs of habitation" — Jan 2018 report notes).
- **MAY2018**: vehicle 1.66 → 2.03 (implemented 2018-05-31). Vehicles are a small share downtown, effect modest.
- **POST2020 — most-misread entry**: NOT a multiplier change. Jan 2020: RTFH stopped using multipliers; DSDP continues. **From 2020 on, comparing DSDP totals to any RTFH/PIT figure is adjusted-vs-unadjusted.** To approximate RTFH basis: individual + tent + vehicle from component rows.

## Other comparability breaks

- **Counting effort**: Downtown Fellowship co-ran counts — 2017: 10/12 months; 2018: 9/12; 2019: 8/12; 2020: 1; 2021–2025: none. `fellowship_month` flag. Live confounder for 2017–2021 trends.
- **Outer Perimeter added April 2021** (null before, not zero; published as 0 in source). 2017 "Outreach Area" was tracked outside the downtown total (supplemental).
- **Block footprint expanded Jan 2022**: Barrio Logan, Golden Hill, Sherman Heights (+121 blocks). ~Half the apparent 2021→2023 increase is footprint. Use Panel261.
- **Core → City Center rename** Jan 2019, retroactive in source.

Source: methodology notes from year-end reports at https://downtownsandiego.org/clean-and-safe/unhoused-care/ (Jan 2018 … Dec 2025; Dec 2025 quoted where fullest).
